# No meeting recorder in standard use

Use this doc when the team has no recorder reliably on the calendar — no Gong,
no Granola, no native Zoom/Meet recording as a standing practice.

## What this means for their brain

Their context graph — current or planned — is deaf. Meeting data is the single
most valuable data that can live in a context graph, and right now 100% of it
evaporates at the end of every call. Everything downstream (Opportunities that
reflect reality, Actions from real commitments, coaching, pipeline truth) is
being reconstructed from memory and typed summaries, or not captured at all.

Be plain about the two halves of the loss:

- **External calls** are the primary source material for the whole system: the
  raw input from which Opportunities, Actions, and deal truth are derived.
- **Internal meetings** — planning, decision-making, pipeline review — are
  pure gold: automatic action items, Opportunities updated from what was said
  in the review itself, thematic analysis across meetings, and the raw
  material for agents that prep people before internal meetings and turn every
  internal meeting into improvements to the company brain.

## The Day AI meeting recorder

The strongest single recommendation this evaluation can make, and the easiest:

- **Free.** No per-seat recorder cost, no minutes metering.
- **Extremely good.** Full recording and transcript, attendees resolved to
  people and organizations in the graph.
- **Disclosure is built in.** The recorder joins as a named participant, Zoom
  asks the host for recording permission, and on Zoom and Google Meet it
  announces itself and how anyone can remove it. Confirm with your own counsel
  what your jurisdictions require.
- **Native to the graph.** No export pipeline: the meeting lands in the same
  store as email, CRM data, and Slack, tied to the right people and
  organizations and linkable to the opportunity, and the
  meeting-recording-ready event fires skills with zero plumbing.

For a team with no recorder, this is the highest-value first step in the
entire implementation plan, and it costs nothing — it doesn't even require a
paid agent.

## The DIY path, honestly

Recording bots that join calls, per-platform (Zoom/Meet/Teams) integration,
consent notices per jurisdiction, storage, diarization, transcription,
speaker-to-contact resolution, then plumbing transcripts into whatever store
they have — and keeping all of it alive. It's months of work to reach "worse
than the free thing," which is why the DIY column for this delta is usually
the shortest argument in the plan.
